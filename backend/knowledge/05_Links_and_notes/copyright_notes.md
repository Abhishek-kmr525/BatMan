# Why some books are not included

I included only PDFs that are official public documents, U.S. government materials, or openly licensed resources.

I did not include unauthorized PDFs of commercial books such as Superforecasting, The Signal and the Noise, Thinking, Fast and Slow, Trading and Exchanges, or Fooled by Randomness. Those books are still useful, but you should access them through a library, official publisher page, or legitimate retailer.

For Forecasting: Principles and Practice, the 3rd edition is free to read online at https://otexts.com/fpp3/. I linked it instead of packaging a copied PDF because I did not find an official downloadable PDF version on the source page.
